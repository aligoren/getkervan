# Code of conduct

This is a short version of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, adapted for this project. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in Kervan a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind and patient with other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at
[conduct@getkervan.dev](mailto:conduct@getkervan.dev). Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly,
and the reporter's privacy is respected. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that
do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior
they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
